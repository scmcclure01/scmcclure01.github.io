Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Home Assistant Nest is a private app used by one person to connect their own Google Nest devices to their own Home Assistant server.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, the app reads Nest device information (such as thermostat temperature, humidity, mode and setpoints) and sends commands to those devices through Google's Smart Device Management API.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to show and control the owner's devices in Home Assistant. It is stored only on the owner's Home Assistant server at home. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
